# Release Notes for October 2025 Update - Version 2.6.282

## Resolved Issues

### Initial Commission - Blank Entity Number

When setting up initial commission rates with a blank entity number (meaning the rate should apply to all entities of the selected type), the system was incorrectly identifying sales as initial or non-initial sales. This has been corrected.

### Initial Commission for All Entities

Additional corrections were made to ensure that initial commission rates with blank entity numbers are evaluated correctly across all sales scenarios.
